# Copy: real, specific, useful

## Principles
- Write for the user's goal in their words: "Manage notifications", not "Configure webhooks".
- Be specific to this product: name the place, offering, customer, number. If a headline fits any competitor, rewrite it.
- Active voice, plain verbs, sentence case, second person. One job per element.
- Consistent names: "Publish" produces a "Published" toast; a "Project" stays a "Project".

## Banned words (use the plain word or cut the sentence)
unleash, unlock, elevate, seamless, effortless, supercharge, turbocharge, empower, leverage, harness, streamline, revolutionize, reimagine, transform (without specifics), game-changer, cutting-edge, next-gen, state-of-the-art, world-class, robust, powerful, innovative, delve, tapestry, landscape, realm, journey, "in today's fast-paced world", "take X to the next level", "the future of X".

## Banned structures
- "Not just X, but Y"; "It's not about X, it's about Y".
- Lists padded to three for rhythm.
- Rhetorical-question headlines ("Tired of slow builds?").
- Em dashes in UI copy. Use a period, comma or colon.
- Exclamation marks in errors or system messages.

## Patterns
- **Hero headline**: what it is plus who it is for, or the concrete outcome. "Invoices that chase themselves, for freelance designers" beats "Elevate your workflow".
- **Subhead**: mechanism or proof in one sentence, with a real number if one exists.
- **CTA**: verb plus object of what happens next: "Start free trial", "Book a 20-min demo", "Download the PDF". Never "Submit", bare "Learn more", or "Get started" when a specific verb exists.
- **Errors**: what happened, then how to fix it. "Card declined by your bank. Try another card or contact your bank." No apologies, no blame, no meaningless codes.
- **Empty states**: what will appear, plus the action that fills it. "No invoices yet. Create your first invoice to see payment status here." + button.
- **Loading**: verb with ellipsis character: "Saving…", "Generating report…".
- **Destructive confirms**: name the thing and the consequence. "Delete 'Q3 roadmap'? This removes 14 tasks for everyone." Buttons: "Delete project", "Cancel".
- **Forms**: noun labels, help text only when needed, placeholders as examples, never as labels.
- **Numbers**: numerals ("8 deployments"), non-breaking space before units ("10&nbsp;MB"), locale formats, tabular figures for comparisons.

## Missing content
- Never lorem ipsum. Write plausible, specific draft copy from available context and mark it `TODO(content)`.
- Never invent testimonials, logos, ratings, user counts, awards or press quotes. Omit the section or add a clearly marked slot.

## Voice
Derive voice from the brand adjectives in DESIGN.md. Write one message in the voice and its opposite to test it. No humor in errors or money flows.
