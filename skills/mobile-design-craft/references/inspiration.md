# Inspiration: borrow techniques, not looks

## Apple Design Awards 2026 (apps)
- **Moonlitt** (Interaction): direct manipulation and a 3D map for moon paths. Study gesture-first data exploration.
- **Tide Guide** (Visuals and Graphics): charts as the hero. Study data-viz type and color.
- **Guitar Wiz** (Inclusivity): Dynamic Type, Increase Contrast, Differentiate Without Color, spoken guidance. Study accessibility as a feature.
- **grug** (Delight and Fun): a hand-drawn style carried into every surface. Study one coherent illustration voice.
- **Primary** (Social Impact): editorial calm for news. Study reading typography.

## By app type
| Type | Study | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| Productivity | Things, Structured, Linear mobile | Quick-add from anywhere, swipe actions, calm density |
| Travel and status | Flighty | Glanceable live status, precise copy |
| Finance | Revolut | Numerals as hero, clear money states, confirmation patterns |
| Habit and learning | Duolingo | One signature character, celebratory haptics (keep streaks ethical) |
| Social and events | Partiful | Playful brand on a standard structure |
| Health | Bevel | Rings and scores with restrained color |
| Wellbeing | Opal | Strong dark aesthetic with one accent |
| Browse and search | Airbnb, Arc Search | Search-first entry, rich card media, shared transitions |

Before borrowing, name the technique and why it fits this app.
